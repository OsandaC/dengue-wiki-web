## Research Question

What does dengue do to the antinuclear antibody (ANA) profile — for whom, by what mechanism, and for how long — and **does acute ANA reactivity have anything to do with the persistent fatigue that follows dengue in roughly one in five patients?** The honest headline answer to the second question, stated up front so nothing below is mistaken for more: **the ANA↔fatigue link is biologically plausible, repeatedly invoked, and untested.** No dataset in this wiki co-measures ANA and fatigue in the same patients over the same window.

## Synthesis

### Part I — Methodological Foundations (assay framework)

Cross-study comparison in this literature is routinely invalidated by **substrate** and **dilution** mismatch. Three rules govern everything below:

- **Substrate.** HEp-2 is the gold-standard IIF substrate class. Rat-liver IIF — used by [[Garcia2009 - Long-term Clinical Symptoms Post-Dengue]] — underestimates ANA, making its 23.1% a conservative floor. See [[Indirect Immunofluorescence ANA Test]].
- **Dilution.** Prevalence falls steeply with dilution (31.7%→13.3%→5.0% at 1:40→1:80→1:160; [[Tan1997 - ANA Range in Healthy Individuals]]). **≥1:80 on HEp-2** is the EULAR/ACR SLE entry criterion ([[Aringer2019 - 2019 EULAR ACR SLE Classification Criteria]]) and the threshold against which dilution-sensitive comparisons in this review are made.
- **IIF vs LIA.** IIF captures all antinuclear reactivity, including germline [[Polyreactive Antibodies|polyreactive IgM]]; LIA tests a defined disease-autoantibody panel. The IIF–LIA gap is the key mechanistic readout (§3.2). [[Chatterjee2024 - ANA Detection in Dengue Kolkata]] ran both IIFA and LIA on its whole cohort, making it the wiki's only acute-dengue source able to separate the specific from the polyreactive fraction.

### Part II — Reference Baselines

**Healthy population (HEp-2 IIF):** ~13–16% at 1:80/>1:100 in US/China data ([[Satoh2012 - ANA Prevalence in United States]] 13.8% at 1:80; [[Dinse2022 - Increasing ANA Prevalence in United States]] 16.1% at 1:80; [[Li2019 - ANA Epidemiology in Chinese Healthy Population]] 14.0% at >1:100), with a ~5–6% floor at ≥1:160 and a documented rising secular trend. Female sex ~2× and age >50 raise the rate. **This is not "the" global healthy baseline**: [[Racoubian2016 - ANA Prevalence in Healthy Lebanese Subjects]] (n=10,814, Lebanon, ≥1:100, HEp-2/Euroimmun AG) reports **26.4%** — higher than the US/China range despite a *stricter* cutoff — and its own Table 1 assembles 11 international cohorts spanning **5.9% (China) to 30.8% (USA African-American)**, with Australia (28.3%), Japan (25.9%), and USA Dallas (25.2%) all comparably high to Lebanon. Healthy-population ANA prevalence varies **~5-fold by ethnicity/geography** (Table 1 does not report cutoffs per cohort, and the China figure is documented elsewhere in the paper at a *stricter* >1:160 threshold than Lebanon's ≥1:100 — see [[Antinuclear Antibodies]] §International Variability for the caveat). See [[Antinuclear Antibodies]] §International Variability in Healthy-Population ANA Prevalence for the full table.

**Generic acute viral infection:** ~21.7% ([[Berlin2007 - Autoantibodies in Nonautoimmune Individuals during Infections]], 8-antigen ELISA), and **transient** — 20.5%→6.4% acute→convalescent in viral hepatitis ([[Codes2002 - Autoantibodies in Acute Viral Hepatitis]]). The default fate of infection-triggered ANA is decay; persistence needs an explanation.

These two baselines bracket the interpretation of every dengue figure: a dengue cohort's control-arm ANA rate must be read against the **wide international healthy-population range** (5.9–30.8%) before being called "elevated," and a dengue-positive rate must clear the **generic acute-viral** baseline (~21.7%) to be considered dengue-*specific* rather than a feature of acute febrile illness generally.

### Part III — ANA in Acute Dengue

#### 3.1 The acute anchor

[[Chatterjee2024 - ANA Detection in Dengue Kolkata]] (n=135 dengue vs. 126 dengue-negative **febrile** controls, HEp-2 IIFA + LIA) is the wiki's primary acute-dengue ANA dataset: **54.8%** IIFA-positive in dengue vs. **10.3%** in febrile controls (p<0.001), with **18.5%** LIA-confirmed vs. **7.1%** in controls. The **febrile** (not healthy) control arm isolates dengue-*specificity* from generic acute-febrile-illness effects — both IIFA and LIA rates remain several-fold higher in dengue than in the febrile comparator. The study pools severity (DF/DHF not separately reported) and provides no sex-adjusted model, so severity- and sex-stratified questions (§3.4, Part IX) must draw on other sources.

#### 3.2 The IIFA:LIA gap — the non-specific (polyreactive) fraction

Chatterjee2024's ~3:1 IIFA:LIA ratio means ~66% of IIFA-positive dengue patients carry **no confirmed disease-specific autoantibody**. The mechanistic account is germline **polyreactive IgM** ([[Zhou2007 - Polyreactive Antibodies Natural Antibody Function]]): low-affinity, multi-specific, IgM-class, nuclear-binding antibodies constitutively present in everyone, amplified by dengue's inflammatory milieu, and expected to be transient (IgM t½ ~8 h). [[Vo2020 - Autoantibody Profiling in Dengue]] converges: 80 IgM vs only 6 IgG autoantibodies elevated — an IgM-dominant breadth signature, not affinity-matured autoimmunity. The operationally meaningful measure of *induced* autoimmunity is the LIA-confirmed fraction (~18.5%), not bare IIFA positivity.

#### 3.3 Disease-category signals in the specific fraction

Chatterjee2024's LIA flagged only **MCTD** (p=0.041) and **autoimmune myositis** (p=0.018) as elevated — wide CIs, n=135, no follow-up; hypothesis-generating. [[Sungnak2025 - Distinct Immune Responses Asymptomatic Symptomatic Dengue]] found total autoantibody *burden* did not differ across asymptomatic/DF/DHF — i.e., severity is encoded in *which* autoantibodies, and in consumption (§3.4, §6), not in total load.

#### 3.4 Autoreactivity does not *rise* with severity — two independent signals

Two independent sources converge on the same pattern — autoreactivity does not increase as dengue severity increases:

1. **[[Chaturvedi2001 - Cytotoxic Factor Autoantibodies DHF]]** — protective anti-hCF in 96% of DF vs 8% of grade-IV DHF (⚠ hCF unvalidated, but a steep gradient — the *firmest* statistical leg).
2. **[[Vo2020 - Autoantibody Profiling in Dengue]]** — primary > secondary IgG autoantibody breadth (primary infection more often presents as DF).

Candidate explanations for why milder disease might carry *more* (not just non-less) autoreactivity: milder/primary disease licensing broad (often non-pathogenic) autoreactivity; *consumption* of nuclear-antigen IgG into immune complexes as DHF worsens (the Vo2020 model, §6) so peak-severity autoreactivity undershoots; and/or infection-order confounding (untested in either source — neither reports primary/secondary serostatus alongside ANA specifically). **What is solid across both is the *absence of a severity-increase*, not a confirmed "milder = more."** That absence is what parallels the severity-*independence* of post-dengue fatigue (§X): the legitimate structural link is a shared **failure to track viral burden**, not a claim that milder disease maximises either. See [[Notable Findings]].

### Part IV — Mechanisms generating ANA in dengue *(carried forward; detail on the concept pages)*

The mechanistic stack established in V2.0 stands unchanged. In brief, with full treatment in [[NS1 Molecular Mimicry in Dengue]], [[Autoimmunity in Dengue]], [[Infection-Triggered Autoimmunity]]:

- **NS1 molecular mimicry** (the dengue-specific arm): anti-NS1 cross-reacts with PDI, vimentin, ATP-synthase-β, HSP60 on platelets/endothelium; PDI epitope mapped to **P311–330** ([[Cheng2015 - NS1 P311-330 Anti-PDI Autoantibodies in DHF]]); foundational IgM anti-platelet autoantibody in [[Lin2001 - IgM Anti-Platelet Autoantibody in Dengue Patients]]. **Critical caveat for the ANA question:** the established mimicry targets are *cytoplasmic/surface* proteins, **not** canonical nuclear antigens — so NS1 mimicry alone cannot explain nuclear IIFA positivity; that requires polyreactive IgM (§3.2) + epitope spreading.
- **Anti-prM** as a second mimicry arm (~60% of dengue B cells; HSP60 cross-reactive; ADE-potent) — [[Dejnirattisai2010 - Anti-prM Antibodies Enhance Dengue ADE]].
- **Bystander activation: likely insufficient** alone — severe COVID-19 ICU patients show no ANA excess over ICU controls ([[Johnson2022 - Infectious Diseases Autoantibodies and Autoimmunity]]).
- **Epitope spreading** — the most plausible route to *persistent*, nuclear-specific post-dengue ANA (cryptic nuclear antigens released by dengue-induced apoptosis/lysis).
- **FcγRIIa-driven IC persistence** — HH genotype impairs IC clearance ([[Bruhns2009 - FcγR Specificity and Affinity for IgG Subclasses]]); associated with post-dengue sequelae (OR 2.83) and DHF (OR 10.56; [[Garcia2010 - Asymptomatic Dengue FcγRIIa Polymorphism]]). A candidate amplifier of *duration*. **This is also the most mechanistically specific bridge to fatigue** (§X): the same IC-persistence pathway Garcia2009 ties to post-dengue symptoms could, in principle, sustain both ANA and fatigue — but no study stratifies ANA by FcγRIIa genotype, and none links genotype to fatigue.
- **Two ANA-*negative* axes:** macrophage-driven MAS/HLH ([[Morel2014 - Autoimmune Response in Children With Dengue]], ANA-negative even when most severe) and the ⚠-unvalidated protective anti-hCF axis ([[Chaturvedi2001 - Cytotoxic Factor Autoantibodies DHF]]). Standard ANA testing is blind to both.

### Part V — The thrombocytopenia bifurcation *(carried forward; full detail in [[Autoimmunity in Dengue]] §V and [[Secondary Dengue Infection]])*

Three immunologically distinct platelet-bound Ig populations: (1) **primary infection** — true IgM anti-platelet *autoantibody* (NS1 mimicry; [[Lin2001 - IgM Anti-Platelet Autoantibody in Dengue Patients]]); (2) **secondary infection** — anti-dengue IgG immune complex (PAIgG; [[Oishi2003 - PAIgG and Thrombocytopenia in Secondary Dengue]]); (3) **secondary infection** — anti-dengue IgM immune complex (PAIgM; [[Saito2004 - PAIgG and PAIgM in Secondary Dengue]]), completely FcγR-independent, DHF-predictive at 92.1% specificity. The same isotype (IgM) is anti-self in primary infection and anti-virus in secondary — a paradox resolvable only by eluate specificity testing. Relevant to the ANA thread chiefly as the reason ANA-negativity never excludes dengue immunopathology, and (via PAIgM's FcγR independence) as the sharpest form of the FcγRIIa paradox.

### Part VI — Temporal dynamics

A coherent timeline:

| Window | Source | Rate | Control |
|---|---|---|---|
| Acute (fever clinic) | [[Chatterjee2024 - ANA Detection in Dengue Kolkata]] | 54.8% IIFA / 18.5% LIA | Febrile 10.3% / 7.1% |
| 6 months | [[Gawali2021 - ANA Prevalence in Seroconverted Dengue Patients]] | 18.33% (AC-1 dominant) | **None** |
| 2 years | [[Garcia2009 - Long-term Clinical Symptoms Post-Dengue]] | 23.1% (rat liver) | None |

The trajectory hint (≈55% acute → ~18% at 6 mo → ~23% at 2 yr) suggests an acute polyreactive spike decaying toward a persistent residual. **Kinetic constraint:** NS1-IgG wanes with t½ ≈ 2.1 y ([[Bos2025 - Longitudinal Antibody Dynamics After Dengue]], preprint), so by 2 years the NS1-mimicry component should be ~halved — meaning Garcia2009's persistence is more attributable to epitope-spreading / IC-persistence than to ongoing NS1 mimicry. Cross-reactive E-IgG, by contrast, *rises* 6–18 months (Bos2025), so different autoreactive components may move in opposite temporal directions. **A notable feature of this table for the fatigue thread: no two ANA points share a follow-up window** — none of them can be aligned to any fatigue trajectory (§X).

### Part VII — Population-level clinical consequences

[[Shih2023 - Autoimmune Disease Risk After Dengue]] (n=63,814 lab-confirmed): after Bonferroni correction across 14 outcomes, **only ADEM** is elevated (aHR 2.72), and **only in month 1**. SLE, GBS, RA, Sjögren's, MG — all non-significant. This refutes the broad-risk claim of [[Li2018 - Increased Risk of Autoimmune Diseases in Dengue]] (whose cohort was only 51.4% lab-confirmed). **Reconciliation with the ANA signal:** ANA positivity ≠ autoimmune disease; most ANA-positive individuals never develop SARD. *This is the central headwind for any "dengue ANA matters clinically" argument — and it applies to the fatigue thread too (§X): elevated acute ANA need not translate into any chronic outcome.* Bidirectional SLE↔dengue diagnostic confusion ([[Farias2024 - Dengue Mimickers]], [[Santosa2012 - Delayed SLE Diagnosis Dengue Serology]]) is the practical hazard in endemic settings; virological confirmation (NS1/RT-PCR) is the anchor.

### Part VIII — The case-report spectrum

Individual-level severity spans from fully **transient** multi-autoantibody syndromes ([[Jardim2012 - Autoimmune Features DHF Case Report]], mitotic-spindle ANA resolving completely) through **ANA-negative MAS** ([[Morel2014 - Autoimmune Response in Children With Dengue]]) to **ANA-positive SLE** with biopsy-confirmed lupus nephritis ([[Rajadhyaksha2012 - Dengue Evolving into SLE and Lupus Nephritis]], [[Velazqueza2017 - SLE vs Dengue Case Series]]). Every SLE case lacks a pre-dengue autoantibody baseline, so **de novo induction vs unmasking is unresolved in all of them**. These rare poles do not contradict the population null; they sit below cohort detection thresholds.

### Part IX — Host determinants

**FcγRIIa genotype** (duration/magnitude of IC stimulation; never measured against ANA rate). **Prior infection history** and **infection order** (Vo2020 primary>secondary IgG inversion). **Acute severity — non-determining:** post-dengue sequelae were *not* predicted by acute DHF/DSS severity in Garcia2009 (p=0.086), post-dengue fatigue was severity-independent in Seet2007 (p=0.855), and acute autoreactivity itself fails to rise with severity in Chaturvedi2001/Vo2020 (§3.4) — three independent "fails to track severity" observations. **Sex — recalibrated (§X):** female excess is real for background ANA ([[Satoh2012 - ANA Prevalence in United States]], [[Dinse2022 - Increasing ANA Prevalence in United States]]) and post-dengue autoimmune markers (Garcia2009, 65.7% vs 36.7%) — but for fatigue specifically, Hertanti2024's pooled estimate (OR 1.65) is *modest*, displacing Seet2007's single-study OR 9.687. Sex is a genuine but smaller lever than V1.x implied, and is a mandatory covariate rather than the explanation.

### Part X — ANA and Post-Dengue Fatigue: an open bridge

This section connects two literatures that have grown up separately — acute ANA in dengue and post-dengue fatigue — and must be read with one fact fixed in place: **no study in this wiki measures ANA and fatigue in the same patients.** What follows is an honest account of what is known about each leg, why the bridge is currently untested, and what would actually test it.

#### 10.1 The fatigue leg

- **[[Seet2007 - Post-Infectious Fatigue Syndrome in Dengue]]** (n=127, Singapore DEN-1): post-infectious fatigue in **24.4% at 2 months**, **severity-independent** (DHF vs DF p=0.855), female sex a predictor. Measured **no** autoimmune markers.
- **[[Hertanti2024 - Fatigue and Post-Infectious Fatigue in Dengue]]** (meta-analysis, 40 studies, 38,406 patients): pooled acute fatigue ~59%, **post-infectious fatigue ~20%** (under high heterogeneity). Lists **autoimmunity as 1 of 4 hypothesised PIF mechanisms** (an estrogen→B-cell→autoantibody→fatigue chain it says "requires further confirmation") and measures **no ANA or autoantibodies**. It recalibrates the female effect to a pooled **OR 1.65 (95% CI 1.27–2.14, I²=0.00)** — the trustworthy quantity, displacing Seet2007's imprecise single-study 9.687.
- **[[Garcia2009 - Long-term Clinical Symptoms Post-Dengue]]** (Cuba, DENV-4, 2-yr): asthenia in **23.0%** of symptomatic patients; ANA-positive in **23.1%** of a 26-patient autoimmune-marker subset. *These two near-identical numbers are a coincidence of different denominators, **not** a correlation* — Garcia2009 does not link ANA to fatigue at the individual level, measured ANA only in symptomatic patients (no non-fatigued comparator), and used rat-liver IIF.

#### 10.2 Why the bridge is currently untested

Three structural gaps:

1. **No co-measurement.** The definitive fatigue paper (Seet2007) measured no ANA; the ANA papers (Chatterjee2024, Gawali2021) measured no fatigue. Garcia2009 measured both but never crossed them at the individual level.
2. **Timepoint mismatch.** Fatigue is assessed at 2 months (Seet2007) or pooled (Hertanti2024); ANA at acute (Chatterjee2024), 6 months (Gawali2021), or 2 years (Garcia2009). **No two ANA/fatigue datapoints share a window.**
3. **The specific-vs-polyreactive problem.** If most acute dengue ANA is transient polyreactive IgM (§3.2), it is exactly the fraction *least* likely to drive a months-later chronic syndrome. A fatigue link would more plausibly run through the LIA-confirmed or IC-persistent fraction — which no acute-dengue ANA study to date has isolated and then followed longitudinally.

#### 10.3 The one legitimate connection: shared severity-independence

The closest the wiki comes to a substantive link is a **structural parallel, not a measurement**: post-dengue fatigue is severity-*independent* (Seet2007, p=0.855), and acute autoreactivity likewise **fails to rise with severity** in two independent sources (§3.4 — Chaturvedi2001, Vo2020). Two post-dengue immune phenomena that both **fail to track viral pathological burden** is a real, citable convergence: it suggests both are governed by **host-intrinsic** factors operating upstream of clinical severity, rather than by how sick the patient got. That is a hypothesis-strengthener for a *common upstream determinant* (candidate: the female-biased, FcγRIIa-modulated, B-cell-tolerance axis) — it is emphatically **not** evidence that ANA causes or marks fatigue. It sharpens the parallel; it does not close it.

#### 10.4 Headwinds (argue uphill, honestly)

- **No chronic autoimmune disease post-dengue** beyond month-1 ADEM (Shih2023) — ANA positivity largely does not convert to disease, so a fatigue link cannot lean on overt autoimmunity.
- **Polyreactive non-specificity** (§3.2) — much dengue ANA may be immunological noise.
- **Existing fatigue mechanisms aren't ANA-mediated** — Seet2007's candidate mechanisms (CD4/CD8 inversion, complement C3a/C5a, HPA-axis) do not require autoantibodies.
- **The "shared female confounder" intuition is weaker than V1.x implied** — Hertanti2024's pooled estimate puts the fatigue female effect at OR 1.65, not Seet2007's single-study OR 9.687. A modest, real sex effect on fatigue does not, by itself, explain dengue's acute ANA elevation (§3.1) — no acute-dengue ANA study in the wiki reports a sex-adjusted model either way. The sharper question survives: *does ANA mark a host-defined subset of fatigue, or is it a parallel bystander of the same upstream biology?*

#### 10.5 What would actually test it

The decisive study would be a **longitudinal dengue cohort co-measuring ANA and fatigue in the same patients**. Minimum design: enrol acute dengue patients — with a **febrile non-dengue control arm** to separate dengue-specific from generic-acute-viral effects — with HEp-2 IIFA **and confirmatory LIA** at enrolment; follow up at 3, 6, and 12 months with a validated fatigue instrument and repeat serology; model fatigue on baseline ANA status/pattern/titre, adjusting for sex, age, and primary/secondary serostatus. Only this design can convert the §10.3 parallel into an individual-level ANA↔fatigue test. Until then, the honest status is: **plausible, parallel, and unproven.**

### Part XI — Vaccine design implications *(carried forward)*

NS1-based constructs retaining **P311–330** will prime anti-PDI cross-reactivity ([[Cheng2015 - NS1 P311-330 Anti-PDI Autoantibodies in DHF]]); the NS1 vaccine paradox (protective anti-NS1 vs pathogenic cross-reactive) is unresolved ([[Wan2012 - Autoimmunity in Dengue Pathogenesis]]). **All current vaccines (CYD-TDV, TAK-003, TV003/TV005) use native prM** and will prime non-neutralising, ADE-potent anti-prM ([[Dejnirattisai2010 - Anti-prM Antibodies Enhance Dengue ADE]]) — the largest unaddressed structural immunogenicity gap. The Katzelnick 1:21–1:80 ADE window ([[Bhatt2020 - Dengue Pathogenesis Review]]) intersects waning vaccine titres. See [[Dengue Vaccine Candidates]], [[prM Protein]].

### Part XII — Acute-phase timing as a distinguishing feature *(carried forward)*

Unlike *C. jejuni*/GBS (2–4 weeks) or EBV/SLE (months–years), dengue NS1-mimicry autoimmunity occurs **during acute viraemia (days 1–7)**, in a predominantly IgM, germinal-centre-independent response ([[Lin2001 - IgM Anti-Platelet Autoantibody in Dengue Patients]], [[Lin2006 - Autoimmune Pathogenesis in Dengue Virus Infection]]). Every acute-ANA dataset in this wiki (§3.1) samples this same window, capturing the acute autoreactive peak — which is exactly why it is the wrong window to detect any *chronic* (fatigue-relevant) autoantibody, and why the longitudinal design in §10.5 is indispensable.

## Epistemic Framework

### Established — convergent across ≥3 independent sources

- **Dengue raises acute ANA well above its own febrile-control baseline.** ~55% IIFA vs. 10.3% in dengue-negative febrile controls, with 18.5% vs 7.1% LIA-confirmed (Chatterjee2024) — converging with elevated rates at 6 months (Gawali2021, 18.33%, no control) and 2 years (Garcia2009, 23.1%, no control).
- **~66% of acute IIFA positivity is non-specific** (polyreactive IgM; IIFA+/LIA−, IgM-dominant) — Zhou2007, Chatterjee2024, Vo2020.
- **Dengue autoimmunity is acute-phase**, not post-infectious — Lin2001/2006/2011, Wan2012, Cheng2015.
- **No broad chronic SARD elevation post-dengue**; only month-1 ADEM — Shih2023 (refuting Li2018).
- **Dengue can cause ANA-negative severe immune complications (MAS)** — Morel2014, Palacios2016.
- **Thrombocytopenia is ≥3 distinct platelet-bound Ig populations** — Lin2001, Oishi2003, Saito2004.
- **NS1 anti-platelet/anti-endothelial autoantibodies are infection-order independent** — Lin2001, Cheng2015, Saito2004, Hung2008.
- **Anti-prM is the dominant structural antibody class (~60%), non-neutralising, ADE-potent; all current vaccines use native prM** — Dejnirattisai2010, Guzman2016.
- **Post-dengue immune sequelae do not *increase* with acute severity (severity-independence).** Severity-independent fatigue (Seet2007, clean null p=0.855), severity-unpredicted autoimmune markers (Garcia2009, p=0.086 borderline), and acute autoreactivity that fails to rise with severity in two independent dengue cohorts (Chaturvedi2001, Vo2020 — §3.4) converge on a *failure to rise* with severity across multiple endpoints. *(It is the severity-**independence** version that reaches Established — anchored on Seet2007's clean null. The stronger "milder disease carries **more** autoreactivity" / non-monotonic version is **not** Established — Chaturvedi2001's gradient is the firmest single leg but is not independently replicated at the same statistical strength; see hypothesis-generating tier.)*

### Probable — ≥2 sources with limitations

- Post-dengue ANA persists to 2 years in selected symptomatic patients (Garcia2009; rat-liver, no control, selected cohort).
- NS1 mimicry is the primary dengue-specific pathogenic mechanism (Lin series, Wan2012, Guzman2016).
- Bystander activation alone is insufficient for dengue ANA (Johnson2022 COVID null).
- Anti-NS1 (hence the NS1-mimicry ANA component) wanes with t½≈2.1 y (Bos2025, preprint).

### Hypothesis-generating — single source / indirect / unproven

- **ANA marks or predicts post-dengue fatigue** — *untested in every wiki source*; supported only by the §10.3 severity-independence parallel and biological plausibility. The thread's central conjecture remains unproven.
- Whether the "autoreactivity does not rise with severity" pattern (Chaturvedi2001, Vo2020 — §3.4) reflects a genuine *inversion* ("milder = more") or simply a *failure to rise*, and whether either reflects true biology vs infection-order confounding (neither source reports primary/secondary serostatus alongside autoreactivity). Chaturvedi2001's gradient is the firmest single leg but is not independently replicated at the same statistical strength.
- Nuclear-antigen IgG **consumption** explains lower ANA in severe DHF (Vo2020, n=8) — would reframe ANA's prognostic meaning if confirmed.
- Primary > secondary IgG autoantibody breadth (Vo2020, n=6, confounded).
- Rising cross-reactive E-IgG drives an opposite-direction anti-coagulation-factor autoreactivity (Bos2025, preprint).
- Dengue-associated ANA-positive SLE is de novo induction vs unmasking (Rajadhyaksha2012, Velazqueza2017, Talib2013 — no pre-dengue baselines).
- MCTD/myositis elevated in the LIA-positive dengue subset (Chatterjee2024, wide CIs).
- Anti-hCF as a protective anti-cytokine axis (Chaturvedi2001 — ⚠ unvalidated).
